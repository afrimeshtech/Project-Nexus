## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The app reads as one coherent product at the token level across all four surfaces - Forest Ground, Price Orange, and the "explain the number in place" copy habit are present everywhere, including the intentionally distinct showcase. The one structural gap: the partner dashboard's desktop nav invented a genuinely good pattern (splitting "work" tiles from "account" links) that its own mobile rendering drops entirely - on the one surface PRODUCT.md is most emphatic is phone-primary for its user.

**Deterministic scan**: 36 CLI findings (all advisory) - 21 design-system-font-size, 9 design-system-color, 5 design-system-radius, 1 codex-grid-background - plus browser-injected findings on 3 of 5 sampled pages (homepage, showcase, /shops; partner and admin were blocked by a tooling-side login stall).

Two claims independently checked rather than taken on faith:
- **Confirmed, worth fixing**: DESIGN.md explicitly states "Don't put an eyebrow above a page heading." Both the homepage (location label above the greeting h1) and /showcase ("Proximity commerce" above the hero h1) do exactly this. DESIGN.md's own Deep Price color entry separately describes "the eyebrow above a page title" as a real usage site, which disagrees with the explicit ban - the documentation contradicts itself here, not just the code.
- **False positive, overriding Assessment B's own verdict**: B flagged a "colored box-shadow glow using #123824" on /showcase as a violation of the glow-halo ban. Traced it to SellerThumb's brand-tile class - the same sanctioned illustrated-tile glow used on every product/seller thumbnail app-wide (recently retuned to Forest Ground this session). DESIGN.md's actual ban targets glow halos behind text/accent elements, not the tile-elevation system, which is the one documented exception to flat design.
- **Genuine, systemic, unflagged until now**: 21 of 36 CLI findings are text-[10px] usage across 6 files. DESIGN.md's type ramp floors out at Eyebrow (11.2px) - no documented step below that.
- **Partner/admin browser evidence honestly incomplete**: blocked by a tooling-side rendering stall; Assessment B explicitly did not fabricate findings for those pages.

## Overall Impression

A coherent, well-built product with real design discipline running through it - the gap isn't inconsistent taste, it's that two good patterns (the partner nav's work/account split, and the "no eyebrow over a heading" rule) don't survive contact with every surface they should apply to.

## What's Working

- The auth-gate `next` chain is genuinely correct end-to-end - requireUser() -> /login?next=... -> the hidden form field -> the post-login redirect - verified in the actual DOM.
- The admin console's ledger-integrity check runs and alerts on every page load, not just in the database - Product Principle 4 made visible in the UI.
- The showcase page's own code comments state "nothing here claims a metric AfriMesh cannot back up" and it holds to that - a real seeded outlet instead of an invented one.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Partner dashboard's mobile nav loses its own information architecture**
- Why it matters: the desktop rail splits "work" tiles from "account" links with a divider; mobile concatenates both into one flat 10-11-item scroll strip - the exact "wall of text" the desktop split was built to avoid, on the retailer's only device.
- Fix: carry the existing work/account grouping into the mobile pill row instead of concatenating them away.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P2] An eyebrow sits above a page heading on two surfaces, against DESIGN.md's own explicit rule**
- Why it matters: homepage and /showcase both violate a documented Don't in exactly the way it anticipated.
- Fix: fold the eyebrow text into the heading's register or drop it into a subtitle; reconcile the Deep Price color entry's conflicting description.
- Suggested command: /impeccable typeset

**[P2] 21 instances of 10px text across 6 files, below DESIGN.md's documented 11.2px floor**
- Why it matters: the largest single finding in the scan, systemic across showcase/page.tsx, partner/api/page.tsx, BarSeries.tsx, OrderBits.tsx, Thread.tsx, ui.tsx.
- Fix: bump to the Eyebrow token (11.2px) or document a smaller step if genuinely needed.
- Suggested command: /impeccable typeset

**[P2] Consequential actions across partner and admin are single-tap with no confirmation**
- Why it matters: clearing a basket, suspending a business, rejecting an application are all one click with no "are you sure," on real accounts and orders.
- Fix: add a confirm step to toggleSuspensionAction, verifyOrganisationAction, and the cart's clear action.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] Reference tables force horizontal scroll on a phone, consistently across partner and admin**
- Why it matters: batch/expiry data, health checks, event logs, and ranking defaults all require sideways scrolling for staff who are never at a desk.
- Fix: a stacked/card-based responsive variant below a breakpoint.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P3] The showcase landing page is thin for a mandatory gate**
- Why it matters: every first-time visitor is routed through here per middleware.ts, yet it offers one escrow claim and no neutral way to dismiss without committing to sign-in or browse.
- Fix: accept as intentionally minimal, or add a section earning its gatekeeper role.
- Suggested command: /impeccable delight
